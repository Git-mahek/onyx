How risk scores work

Scores range from 0 to 100. The score rises with impossible readings, rapid rate-limiting trips, and open ports detected during scanner runs. It gradually decays over time as the node behaves normally.

0 to 39: Low risk Verified TLS connections, readings within nominal boundaries.
40 to 69: Medium risk Unusual gradients or unauthenticated service ports discovered.
70 to 100: High risk Rejected impossible packets, flood DoS triggers, or device timeout.
